# Communication and direction

Situation: idea-hackathon demonstration of 톡톡, a cafe voice-and-touch ordering MVP for older or kiosk-unfamiliar customers, presented live and readable alone. Audience: hackathon judges and participants. Outcome: understand the ordering problem hypothesis, the “톡톡” idea and its name, and the MVP journey; end with the idea recap and Q&A, never a pilot or rollout request. No unverified-item warnings and no further-validation slide. CONTENT.md and the manifest are canonical for copy; 17 slides, 16:9, no speaker notes.

Name: the first “톡” is the sound of touching a screen; the second means conversational “Talk”. Voice adds to touch, it does not replace it. Both approved lines stay: “말하면, 주문이 됩니다.” and “점원에게 말하듯, 편하게 주문하세요”.

# Visual system: warm cafe paper, editorial composition

Light mode. Tokens: background #FBF6EE (cream paper), foreground #291F19, surface #FFFFFF (raised white panels), muted #6B5F57, accent #EC4613 (orange), green #22774F, border #E2D8CB, soft #F5F1EA. Font IBM Plex Sans KR 400/700 for display and body.

Type: content titles 40pt/700, one line, shared frame (x=120, y=150, 1560×110); 16pt section eyebrow; body 22–23pt (cards 20pt, minimum 19pt); key statements 26–34pt; source captions 16pt; cover title 96pt with 30pt slogans; numerals are hierarchy peaks (slide 2 “톡톡” 64pt, slide 9 total 46pt, slide 10 “3 → 2” 88pt, slide 16 “6” 160pt). Copy, explicit line breaks and font sizes are fixed; text frames are never narrowed.

Structure: margin 120; header rule and eyebrow at the top, title at y=150, content begins near y=320–340 and ends by about 960, footer rule 1000. A single orange edge bar (18 units) runs down the left of every slide (cream on the green closing slide). Panels are tinted rectangles with a 6-unit top strip (orange, or green for green-tone panels); solid green panels are flat emphasis blocks.
